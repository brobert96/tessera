Tessera

A player for the music on your own server.

Getting started

Tessera does not come with music. It plays what you keep on a music server you run yourself, or one somebody shares with you.

  1. Open Tessera and type your music server's address, user name and password. Tessera works with Navidrome and other Subsonic-compatible servers.

Want to try it first? Sign in with Navidrome's public demo server: address demo.navidrome.org, user name demo, password demo.

Common questions

Tessera says the server needs an https address. A server you reach over the internet must use https. Plain http works only on your home network.

Podcasts and Books. Off by default. If you run an Audiobookshelf server, switch them on under Settings > Tabs and connect it there.

Where is my data? On your phone and on your own servers. Tessera has no account and sends nothing to us. See the privacy policy.
