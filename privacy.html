Privacy policy

Tessera · last updated 22 November 2026

Tessera does not collect any data about you. We have no servers that the app talks to, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

The addresses of the servers you connect to, your user names and passwords (kept in the iOS Keychain), your settings, the queue, the songs and episodes you download, and a cache of covers. Deleting the app removes all of it.

What goes to your own servers

Tessera talks only to the servers you sign in to. They receive what any player sends them: your sign-in, what you search for and play, your place in podcasts and audiobooks, files you choose to upload (books, or a listening-history file), and, if your server offers notifications, the token Apple uses to deliver them to this phone. Those servers are run by you or by whoever gave you the login; what happens there is up to them.

Podcast episodes may be downloaded straight from the address where the show publishes them. That site sees an ordinary download request, as it would from any podcast app.

Apple

If you allow it in iOS Settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with developers. These do not identify you.

Children

Tessera collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date. Questions: info@wanderframestudios.com.
